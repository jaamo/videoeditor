# hidasta: findings (camera 2026-11-08 08.42.00.mp4)

## What it is

A talk to camera for everyday cyclists, in Finnish. In almost every traffic situation you can either speed up or slow down, and slowing down is almost always the safer choice. He stands by his bike in a hi-vis jacket and helmet, on an overcast day with bare trees behind.

The camera is 2160×3840 (vertical 4K), 29.93 fps, 1:30.2 long. It's one continuous take with no retakes.

**How it differs from the previous take** (2026-11-06; topic: "slowing down beats speeding up in bike traffic"):
- **Same message, but rewritten.** It's more formal Finnish: "minä" and "Pyöräillessä" instead of "mä" and "niinku".
- **New examples.** It adds three concrete cases: fitting past someone, making the lights, taking a bend.
- **Order of the options.** It says "kiihdyttää" first and "hidastaa" second; the old take had them the other way round.
- **Near misses in the first person.** "minä itse asiassa lähdin kiihdyttämään" replaces "kun sä lähdet kiihdyttämään sen sijaan, että hidastaisit".
- **The disclaimer is an idiom now.** "en ole itse puhdas pulmunen" replaces "en ole itse mikään täydellinen".
- **New ending.** It ends on "vahva suositus vauhdin hidastamiselle" instead of "hidastaminen kannattaa".
- **No "sataprosenttisesti" hook.** The thesis is now "Käytännössä joka kerta turvallisempi ja järkevämpi vaihtoehto on hidastaa".
- **Cleaner take.** There are no retakes; the old take had five of the closing line. The only loss of thread is 62.45–73.1.
