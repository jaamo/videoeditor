# voitelu: findings

## What it is

A Finnish talking-head video for the Kampiapina YouTube channel on bicycle chain lubrication ("mun virallinen kanta polkupyörän ketjujen voitelusta"): his history with Muc-Off wet lube, Rex Black Diamond drip wax, 42 Shield ceramic coating on the road bike, and Squirt waxes he got to test; ends asking for comments. One A-roll file, P1004769.MOV (16:55.5, Lumix, 3840×2160, 23.976 fps, flat/log look, lav mic), framed chest-up against a white wall/door, orange hoodie, flat cap. Three b-roll files, all Lumix 4K 23.976, hands holding the products against the same wall. Timeline 23.976 fps.
