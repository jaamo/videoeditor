# basic-interview: findings

## What it is
A short Finnish demo (about 4 min raw) of Outerview for people who might use it. Jaakko enters his own topic, Maya starts the interview, he opens the drafts made from an interview, shortens a LinkedIn post through the chat, and opens the publish panel. The video ends with a wrap-up on how to use it every week. It has a phone face camera (main, 1080x1920) and a 1920x1080 screen recording (it starts at -3.81 s; sync confidence 13.7, checked by eye: it lines up).

## Intro and outro
- **Intro** on `face`: 21.65–26.4 (handheld close-up, "Moikka… Outerview-tuotetta"), then 30.1–34.85 (desk framing, "Tämä on siis tuote…"). The framing jumps between the two, which reads as a normal cut. Switch to `screen` at 35.0 ("Etusivu näyttää meille tältä").
- **Outro** on `face`: 207.65–222.6 (second take plus "Aika toimiva systeemi…", desk framing), then 230.1–236.0 ("Kiitos kun kuuntelit… vähän myöhemmin", handheld close-up, smile at 235.9). The body should end on `screen` at 193.1 or 196.1 ("Tällä tavalla toimii" fits either side).
- **Face framing**:
  - Close-ups (21.6–27, 229.6–236): face in the upper half, eyes at about 35% height. Very good for the middle layout and fine in the corner.
  - Desk framing (29–228): camera low and a little to his left. The top of his head is 1–5% from the top edge (tight), eyes at about 20–25%, the lower half is hoodie, desk and gesturing hands.
  - In the middle it's acceptable. In the 400 px corner his face is about 75 px wide with the head at the top edge: small but recognisable.
  - During the demo he mostly looks to the side at the laptop, which is natural in the corner.
